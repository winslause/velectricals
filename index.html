❓ GOT QUESTIONS? WE'VE GOT ANSWERS ❓

Honest Answers to Your Electrical Questions

No jargon. No surprises. Just straight talk about what to expect when you work with Viobix Electricals.

Will you make a huge mess I have to clean up?

The honest answer: We leave your space cleaner than we found it.

Our team uses drop cloths, magnetic door seals on electrical panels, and HEPA-filtered vacuums to contain dust. We do a complete cleanup before leaving — including wiping down surfaces and removing all debris. If there's any mess, call us within 24 hours and we'll send someone back at no charge.

Included in every job, no extra fee

Do you offer fixed-price quotes, or is it time & materials?

The honest answer: Fixed price for 90% of our work — no hourly surprises.

After our free site assessment, we provide a fixed-price quote for most residential and commercial jobs (panel upgrades, wiring, solar installations, etc.). You approve the price before we start — what we quote is what you pay. For complex troubleshooting, we may suggest time & materials, but you approve ALL work in writing before we proceed.

Written quotes provided before any work begins

What brand of parts do you use? (Why not the cheapest?)

The honest answer: We use premium brands because cheap parts fail — and that costs you more.

We primarily use Siemens, Schneider Electric, and Legrand components. Yes, they cost 20-30% more than generic brands — but they last 3x longer. Cheap breakers can overheat, cheap wires degrade faster, and cheap panels fail inspections. We stand behind our work with a 2-year warranty, so it's in our interest to use parts that won't fail.

✓ Siemens Breakers ✓ Schneider Electric Panels ✓ Legrand Wiring Devices

What happens if something breaks a month later?

The honest answer: We fix it — no questions asked.

All our work comes with a 2-year warranty on labor and manufacturer warranties on parts (typically 5-10 years). If something stops working properly within that period, call or text our aftercare line directly — not an automated 1-800 number. We'll schedule a return visit within 48 hours. No diagnostic fees, no arguing about fault. Just fixed.

Direct line to our warranty department +254 769 525 570

How do I know you're safe around my kids/pets?

The honest answer: Safety is our #1 priority — for your family and our team.

All our electricians undergo criminal background checks and safety training (OSHA/NFPA 70E). On-site protocols include: barrier tape around work areas, lock-out/tag-out procedures, and no live work unless absolutely necessary. We also keep all tools and materials secured and out of reach of children/pets. We're happy to explain our safety plan before we start.

All team members carry photo ID and safety certifications

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

The honest answer: Within 30-60 minutes for Nairobi emergencies.

We have 4 emergency response vehicles stationed across Nairobi (CBD, Westlands, Industrial Area, and Embakasi). For urgent calls (power outage, sparking outlets, burning smell), we aim to arrive within 30 minutes during business hours and 60 minutes overnight. We'll give you an exact ETA when you call.

24/7 emergency dispatch: +254 769 525 570

Do you handle permits and KPLC approvals?

The honest answer: Yes — we handle everything from start to finish.

As a KPLC authorized contractor, we manage all necessary permits, inspections, and utility coordination. This includes: building department permits, KPLC meter installation applications, and final inspection sign-offs. We provide you with copies of all approved permits and certificates. You don't need to visit any government office.

Full permit management included in our quotes

Do you offer payment plans or financing?

The honest answer: Yes — flexible options available for major installations.

For projects over KSh 100,000 (solar systems, full rewiring, panel upgrades), we offer 50% deposit, 50% upon completion. We can also recommend financing partners for larger installations. Smaller jobs (repairs, outlets, lighting) require payment upon completion. We accept MPESA, bank transfer, and cash.

MPESA payments accepted (Lipa Na MPESA)

No question is too small or too technical. Call, WhatsApp, or email us — we respond within minutes, not hours.

System Testing & Commissioning

Before declaring any project complete, we conduct comprehensive testing to verify that every system operates safely, efficiently, and as designed. Our testing protocols exceed minimum code requirements.
