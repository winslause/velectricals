System Testing & Commissioning
Rigorous Validation for Safety & Performance

Before declaring any project complete, we conduct comprehensive testing to verify that every system operates safely, efficiently, and as designed. Our testing protocols exceed minimum code requirements.

Our Testing Protocol Includes:
  • Continuity Testing: Verification of all circuit connections and grounding
  • Insulation Resistance: Megger testing to ensure proper wire insulation
  • Voltage Testing: Verification of correct voltage at all outlets and devices
  • Phase Balance: Testing of three-phase systems for proper load distribution
  • GFCI/AFCI Testing: Verification of ground fault and arc fault protection
  • Load Testing: Verification of system performance under operating conditions
  • Thermal Imaging: Infrared scanning to detect hot spots and connection issues
  • Documentation: Comprehensive test reports with results and certifications
✓ Independent Verification: For critical systems, we can arrange third-party engineering inspection and certification for additional assurance.
